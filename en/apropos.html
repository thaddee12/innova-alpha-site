Our Mission

Putting technology in its place

A website, software or AI tool does not create value on its own. It accelerates an organization that works, and amplifies the disorder of one that doesn't. That is why our work starts with the organization.

We support businesses and institutions in Central Africa, from SMEs to established groups. The framework is the same for all: only the scale changes.

Our signature

The Alpha of Digital

Alpha is the first letter of the Greek alphabet. For us, it first means the beginning: every transformation starts with a diagnostic, never with a tool.

It also expresses an ambition: to become the benchmark for digital transformation and AI in Cameroon, then across the region. A benchmark is not proclaimed, it is proven, project after project.

Finally, it stands for a method: bringing order to decisions, dealing first with what matters and putting digital back in service of performance.

What we commit to

Our working rules

01

Diagnosis before proposal

We don't quote anything before seeing how you work. The first consultation costs nothing and commits you to nothing; the Alpha Diagnostic is deducted if you continue.

02

Saying what is not urgent

A project set aside is a budget saved. We say so, even when it costs us a sale.

03

Everything explained clearly

No imposed technical jargon. Every decision is justified in time saved or costs avoided.

04

We stay after delivery

Training, documentation and follow-up are part of the project. You leave with your access and your source files.
