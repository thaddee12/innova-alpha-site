Client case · 01

AQUAFARM: making visible an expertise nobody could find.

A fish farm that mastered its production, sold to demanding professionals, and yet remained impossible for them to find.

Fish farming · Mfou, 30 km from Yaoundé

SCOOPS founded in 2020

2.5 months · contract renewed

aquafarmcameroun.cm↗
Immersion step

The situation

Founded in 2020 by Louis Charles Meka, the farm produces catfish fingerlings and does it well: 270,000 fingerlings per cycle, 95% survival rate. On visibility, nothing organized: no visual identity, no website, a Facebook page with over 2,300 followers left abandoned.

Strategy step

The challenge

AQUAFARM sells directly to professional fish farmers and wholesalers, who check a supplier's reliability before placing orders of several thousand fingerlings. There was open ground: searches such as “alevins Cameroun” and “pisciculture Mfou” had almost no well-ranked player.

Transformation step

Our intervention

Four work streams over two and a half months: complete rebranding, sales brochure, SEO website, digital rollout. The 2026 Edition brand guidelines set a sober, technical blue identity worthy of the trade. The website was deployed on aquafarmcameroun.cm with complete technical SEO from launch.

What we delivered

  • Complete brand identity: logo, palette, stationery and textile applications
  • Website deployed in production, six pages, mobile-optimized
  • Schema.org structured data: product page, reviews, local business
  • Active, optimized Google Business Profile
  • Maintenance and community management renewed after delivery (Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn)
Performance step

The result

The website is approved and in production. The Google Business Profile shows a 4.5-star rating. AQUAFARM went from no digital presence to an ongoing business relationship with INNOVA ALPHA, well beyond the initial one-off delivery.

Client case · 02

KASHOP: an online catalog that sells through WhatsApp.

Two shops, a wide catalog, and sales that depended entirely on walk-in customers.

Electronics · Yaoundé

Two physical shops

Represented by Anicet Kenne

kashop.cm↗
Immersion step

The context

KASHOP distributes electronics in Yaoundé, through two physical shops. Phones, chargers, audio, security, car accessories, spare parts: a wide catalog, with no online presence. Sales depended entirely on walk-in customers and word of mouth.

Strategy step

The challenge

Build a real online catalog, without online payment: customers mostly order through WhatsApp and pay cash or by Mobile Money on delivery. The goal: be visible on Google among the country's best electronics shops, against Glotelho, the dominant player since 2000.

Transformation step

The INNOVA ALPHA solution

A static catalog website, with no database or CMS, driven by a product data file: it stays fast on limited mobile connections. The purchase flow is simple: catalog, cart, prefilled WhatsApp message to the KASHOP number. Built from the client's mockup, the website was enhanced with features that were not in the initial brief.

What we delivered

  • 5-page website (home, catalog, order, about, contact), deployed in production on kashop.cm
  • Catalog of 37 real products with cart, favorites and detailed product pages
  • Delivery fee calculator using an interactive map based on distance from the shop
  • Order tracking and ordering flow entirely through WhatsApp
  • Complete technical SEO: Schema.org structured data (store and product pages), sharing tags optimized for WhatsApp and Facebook
Performance step

Result

The website is live and already ranks well on direct searches (“kashop”, “KASHOP Yaoundé”). Work continues on the Google Business Profile and local SEO, to capture specific searches for the products sold rather than targeting the sector's long-standing leaders right away.
