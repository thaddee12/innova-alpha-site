FAQ

A five-step approach: immersion, strategy, transformation, AI, performance. It begins with a three-part diagnostic: executive interview, operational analysis, document and digital audit.

A written report and a prioritized roadmap: what costs, what pays, what can wait. Support continues if you decide so.

No. The roadmap belongs to you, with or without us.
